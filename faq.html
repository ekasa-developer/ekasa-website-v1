What is EKASA?

EKASA is a personality development and self-discovery program by Arch Point Wellness. It blends ancient sciences with modern psychological and neurological tools to help you understand your strengths, mindset and potential, so you can make clearer choices in studies, career, relationships and everyday life. Science, Soul & Self in Harmony.

What are the benefits of EKASA and how does it help?

EKASA gives you a clear picture of who you are and how you naturally think, learn and respond. With that clarity you can understand your strengths, choose direction with confidence, improve focus and emotional balance, communicate more easily, support children in the way that suits them best, and get a practical personalised report and guidance.

Is it confidential?

Yes. Your sessions, assessment results and reports are kept private and are shared only with you (or with a parent or guardian in the case of a child). We never share personal details or results with any third party without your consent.

What workshops does EKASA offer?

EKASA workshops are interactive, practical sessions for individuals, families, schools and groups. They include personality, parenting and mindfulness workshops. To enquire, contact contact@ekasa.in or +91 96943 00555.

Who is this for?

Anyone aged 5 and above. EKASA is designed for school students, college students, working professionals and parents. Programs are adapted to the age and needs of each person.

What is EKASA Seed?

EKASA Seed is the starting point of the EKASA journey. It is a guided assessment of about one hour that gives you a clear first picture of your personality, strengths and natural tendencies. You receive your report within 2 to 3 working days. Pricing: price on enquiry.

What is EKASA Dost?

EKASA Dost is our counselling service, a supportive space to talk, be heard and find clarity. Sessions are available online and in person, and everything you share is confidential. Pricing: price on enquiry.

What is EKASA Flower?

EKASA Flower is the deeper stage of the journey. It builds on the Seed foundation with a fuller, more detailed assessment, a comprehensive personalised report, and one-to-one guidance. Pricing: price on enquiry.
